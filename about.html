Hi, I’m Nilotpal.

A final-year Multimedia Communication & Design student at CIT Kokrajhar, Assam, and a freelance designer and developer.

I started out taking apart gadgets to see how they worked, and ended up designing the screens people use to control them. Today I split my time between research and interface design, and writing the frontend code that ships it.

For me, design sits at the sweet spot between engineering and marketing. You can't just solve a problem. You also introduce the product in a way that it sits in the customer's mind. Solving a problem doesn't mean joining wires. It can be sketching a line.
